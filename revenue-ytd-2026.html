Four things to check before this goes out

issues in the data, not in the trading
1

September is open and its numbers are not usable

The Sep 2026 rows report group revenue of $6.0m against gross profit of $11.4m — GP above revenue, which cannot happen. ANZ and Bulb both show negative revenue for the month, and EMEA and Asia both show GP exceeding revenue. This is a mid-close state, so the whole month is excluded here. Anything quoting a nine-month YTD from this model right now is wrong.

2

The budget curve nearly triples across the year

Monthly budget runs $17.7m–$19.0m from January to April, then steps to $27.2m, $34.8m, $45.5m and $51.1m. Actual revenue grew steadily over the same stretch — $20.5m to $27.7m — so the shortfall is almost entirely phasing, not a collapse in trading. Worth confirming the back-half budget was loaded at the right grain before the variance is presented as a performance miss.

3

Group EBITDA is carrying $12.1m of unallocated cost

The Global region has zero revenue and −$12.1m of EBITDA for the period — corporate overhead that sits outside every regional line. It is the reason group EBITDA is −$8.2m while ANZ (+$6.0m) and Downstream (+$4.0m) are both positive. The regional EBITDA percentages in the table above are therefore pre-allocation.

4

Six studios have no budget loaded at all

Life Science UK, Life Science CH, London, Milan, Downstream US and Downstream AMS carry $39.0m of actual revenue between them against a budget of zero, so their variance reads as pure upside. Separately, Medical holds a budget of −$33.3m with no revenue, which nets against the group budget. Both distort the variance total.

How this was built

Semantic model  Monthly Business Review  ·  workspace: BI for AI
Table           Income_Stat
Revenue         [Total_Revenue]        = CALCULATE( SUMX( Income_Stat,
                                          [Revenue] * [Conversation] ),
                                          [TimePeriod] = "MTD" )
Budget          [Total_Revenue_budget] — same shape on [RevenueBudget]
Period          AsAtMonth in Jan–Aug 2026 (Sep excluded, see flag 1)
  • The raw Revenue column is in each entity's local currency — INR, AUD, EUR, GBP and so on — so it cannot be summed across regions. Conversation is the FX factor to USD, and the model's own measures apply it. Every figure here is USD.
  • TimePeriod holds MTD, QTD and YTD copies of the same rows. The measures filter to MTD; summing the column without that filter triple-counts.
  • Region uses New_Region. Studio uses Studio_Location — the Studio column reads "Local" for every row and is not a usable grain.
  • GP% and EBITDA% are calculated on actual revenue for the period, not taken from the model's stored margin columns.
  • Rounding is applied at display only; totals are summed from unrounded values, so a column may differ from its printed parts by 0.1.
