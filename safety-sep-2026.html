How to read the rates

TRIFR is the total recordable injury frequency rate: lost-time plus medically-treated injuries per one million hours worked, on a rolling twelve-month basis to the period end. LTIFR, MTIFR and FAIFR are the same construction for lost-time, medically-treated and first-aid injuries respectively. Report-only and refused-care events are not counted in any rate. All figures on this page recompute for the selected scope, so a studio's rate uses only that studio's injuries and that studio's hours.

Rates on small hour bases move a lot from a single event, so bars for scopes with fewer than 100,000 hours in the twelve-month window carry a low exposure tag and are drawn muted. Read those as counts, not rates.

Four things to check before this goes out

Regional rates in the Power BI page are diluted. In the Monthly Business Review model, Fact_WorkHoursByMonth has no relationship to Dim_Project, so slicing the Safety page by region divides regional injuries by group hours. APAC, for example, shows a TRIFR of about 4.5 in the dashboard against 8.4 here. The fix is a single relationship on ProjectId; this page joins hours to projects directly so its regional figures are internally consistent.
Sandbox and training projects are excluded here but not in the dashboard. Three test projects (zzSandbox Test Project, zzEMEA Sandbox Test, *Sandbox Training Project*) hold two recordable and two first-aid events plus 864 hours. They are removed from every figure on this page, so group totals will sit slightly below the dashboard until the model filters them too.
Injury detail is incomplete. Half of injury records have no harm source and four in ten have no work activity recorded; body part is almost never filled. The breakdown charts show these as “Not recorded” rather than dropping them.
Near-miss counts include observations. The near-miss table mixes incident-type “Incident” and “Observation” records; the type split is available in the near-miss breakdown. The Americas report far more near misses per hour than other regions, which is a reporting-culture signal as much as a hazard one.

How this was built

Source: the Monthly Business Review semantic model in the Company Metrics – DEV workspace, read on . Monthly injury counts and work hours come from Fact_InjuryByMonth and Fact_WorkHoursByMonth at project-month grain (October 2023 to September 2026), joined to Dim_Project and Dim_Country for region, country and studio. Event-level detail comes from Fact_Injury with Dim_IncidentItem, and from Dim_NearMiss. Rolling twelve-month rates are computed in the page from those rows, so every filter and drill re-derives the numbers rather than reading a stored measure. No external libraries; the page is a single self-contained file.
